Glossary
Rankings refresh automatically once a day, based on your league's own scoring rules
Superflex
A lineup slot that can be filled by a QB, RB, WR, or TE - unlike a normal FLEX, it allows a 2nd starting QB, which makes QBs much more valuable.
Half-PPR
Each catch (reception) is worth 0.5 points, regardless of yardage - a middle ground between no points-per-catch and a full point per catch.
FAAB / Waivers
How free agents get picked up during the season. Your league uses rolling waivers - whoever picks a player up moves to the back of the priority line.
Replacement level
The points a "typical" bench-level player at that position would score. A player's "value" is how far above that baseline they are - not just their raw points.
Bye week
One week each season where an NFL team doesn't play. Any of your players on that team can't score points that week.
Boom/bust
A player whose weekly scores swing wildly - huge point totals some weeks, very little others - instead of scoring close to the same amount every week. Higher risk, higher ceiling.
Trend (gut check)
Compares a player's last 3 games to their season average - helps tell whether a recent bad (or great) week is a real trend or just one weird game.
Consistency rating
Based on how much a player's actual weekly score swung last season. "Consistent" players deliver close to their average every week; "Boom/bust" players have huge highs and lows.
VORP
"Value Over Replacement Player" - the method this app uses to rank players by how much better they are than someone easily available, not just raw stats.
